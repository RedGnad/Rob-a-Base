# Attributions

Everything in `assets/` (models, sounds, textures, icons, avatar clips) is generated by
the scripts in `tools/`, with the exceptions listed below. The pistol, `assets/Models/gun.glb`, comes from the open Decentraland
model catalog (`models.dclregenesislabs.xyz`).

## Jump and glider glyphs

`tools/ui/vendor/joypad/double-jump-normal.svg` and `glide-normal.svg` are copied verbatim
from the Decentraland mobile explorer, https://github.com/decentraland/godot-explorer
(path `godot/src/ui/components/organisms/joypad/`, main branch, fetched 4 September 2026),
licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0. They are rasterised by
`tools/ui/build-thumb-icons.py` so the scene's jump disc shows exactly the pictures the client
would have shown. Details in `tools/ui/vendor/joypad/NOTICE.md`.

## Particle sprites

`assets/textures/fx-*.png` are built by `tools/vfx/build-particle-textures.py` from six
sprites of Kenney's Particle Pack 1.1 (https://kenney.nl/assets/particle-pack, `star_06.png`,
`smoke_04.png`, `flame_05.png`, `circle_02.png`, `trace_06.png` and `twirl_02.png`,
black-background versions, downloaded 2 November 2026), released under Creative Commons Zero
(CC0). The sources and the licence file ship in `tools/vfx/vendor/kenney-particle-pack/`.
Credit is not required; it is given here because the pictures are Kenney's.

## Raid boss monsters

`assets/Models/boss-bluedemon.glb`, `boss-mushroomking.glb` and `boss-yeti.glb` are the Blue
Demon, the Mushroom King and the Yeti of Quaternius' Ultimate Monsters
(https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemonsters.html, "License CC0", October 2022), unmodified.
They were downloaded on 3 November 2026 as GLB from the author's own uploads on Poly Pizza
(https://poly.pizza/m/S7jYW6Amye, https://poly.pizza/m/grnFTziU8u and
https://poly.pizza/m/ceRHrn8HHE, licence "CC0 1.0"), because the author's Google Drive refused
the download ("Quota exceeded"). Credit is not required; it is given here because the models are
Quaternius'.
